# Phase 1 Plan 01: Live World Coordinate Tracking & Zoom Dollying

## Objective
- **What:** Update `SolarSystemCanvas.tsx` and `PlanetMesh.tsx` so that camera tracking targets the actual revolving 3D world position of planets rather than a fixed X-axis coordinate, and make Zoom In/Out dolly along the camera viewing axis instead of resetting the camera to default overview coordinates.
- **Why:** Satisfies VIS-01 and VIS-02. When users click a planet in the 3D Solar System, the camera must dynamically follow the revolving world in real time. Zoom buttons must intuitively zoom without triggering an abrupt reset.
- **Output:** Fully functional live 3D planetary tracking and camera dollying.
