# Phase 4 Plan 01: Storage Validation & Video Iframe Sandboxing

## Objective
- **What:** Add type guard validation, max entry capping (20 entries), and quota error resilience to `localStorage` in `MissionDesignerPage.tsx`, and add sandbox permissions and domain validation to video iframes in `ApodSection.tsx`.
- **Why:** Satisfies DATA-01 and DATA-02. Protects the application from corrupted browser storage, quota exhaustion errors, and malicious external video embeds.
- **Output:** Hardened client-side persistence and secure third-party media embeds.

<task type="code">
  <name>Harden localStorage and sandbox video iframes</name>
  <files>src/pages/MissionDesignerPage.tsx, src/components/discovery/ApodSection.tsx</files>
  <action>
    1. In `src/pages/MissionDesignerPage.tsx`, define a type guard `isValidSavedMission(item: any): item is SavedMission` verifying id, name, departure, target, deltaV, and timestamp fields.
    2. When reading from `localStorage`, filter items through the type guard and cap length at 20.
    3. In `handleSaveMission`, cap saved missions at 20 entries, wrap `localStorage.setItem` in a try/catch block with quota fallback (trimming to 10 entries if quota is exceeded).
    4. In `src/components/discovery/ApodSection.tsx`, validate that video URLs belong to trusted domains (`youtube.com`, `youtube-nocookie.com`, `player.vimeo.com`, or `.nasa.gov`), and add `sandbox="allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-presentation"`.
  </action>

  </verify>
